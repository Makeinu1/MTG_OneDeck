# Audio-visual contract

<!-- clause: AV-001 -->
Audio and motion are presentation of successful semantic events. Pointer, touch, keyboard, drag, menu, and focus events do not emit a cue by themselves.

<!-- clause: AV-002 -->
BGM transport and semantic SFX are independent policies. Theme and route controls determine effective audibility without rewriting saved preferences. Light-theme silence, reduced-motion behavior, beat scheduling, and non-blocking presentation are preserved.

<!-- clause: AV-003 -->
One bulk semantic action produces one corresponding event and one cue policy. The commander cast is the explicit ritual boundary; ordinary actions use the ordinary semantic cue path. The state transition completes before any cue is scheduled.

<!-- clause: AV-004 -->
Human audibility and comfort are checked only in the AV manual scenario. Mechanical event, manifest, and integration checks remain deterministic and offline.
