# OneDeck delivery policy

## Status and authority

This is the proposed current delivery authority for OneDeck. It owns **how
progress is produced and judged** inside an authorized product envelope. It
does not redefine the product position, player-visible feature scope,
acceptance thresholds, detailed governance mechanics, or external authority.
Those remain respectively with the product requirements and existing
document-governance authorities.

This policy does not authorize commits, pushes, deployment, publication,
shipment, or any other external or irreversible action.

## Outcome-first progress

Measure progress by observable player journeys and their quality, not by
substrate, governance artifacts, audit volume, or tests in isolation. Report
supporting governance, audit, telemetry, and substrate work separately from
player-visible progress.

The user owns Goal, Done when, scope, acceptance quality, North-Star changes,
and irreversible or external authority. Within that envelope, the delivery
system chooses models, effort, tools, layout details, prototypes,
implementation mechanics, repair work, and audit allocation.

## Design ownership and prototype evidence

UI design is a delivery responsibility, not a request for pixel-by-pixel user
approval. Use appropriate product-design and playtest skills, observable
references, real-size prototypes, and independent fresh-context visual review
to establish choices before implementation.

Treat exact area ratios, breakpoints, density thresholds, timing, and
transition values as prototype-derived variables. Do not promote them to a
product requirement without the authority to change that contract.

## Production proof before broad refinement

First prove a short, deterministic production scenario that covers:

- land play and casting;
- HOLD, response, and resolution;
- combat;
- a secret choice;
- an honest manual fallback;
- reconnect, elimination, and winner.

Use this proof to expose core player-journey failures before broad refinement.
It is a product evidence sequence, not a replacement for the detailed test or
release procedure held by document governance.

## Targeted iteration and risk-scoped assurance

While iterating, run only the targeted checks needed to validate the changed
claim. Freeze a candidate before one independent, risk-scoped audit. After
concrete findings are repaired, repeat only evidence that the finding or
repair invalidated. Run one final full check on the resulting frozen candidate.

Keep implementation, judgment, and cold audit separate. A cold auditor uses
fresh context, edits nothing, and returns findings for the authorized owner to
adjudicate. Detailed candidate fingerprints, role routing, check commands,
and release gates remain governed by the existing document-governance
authority rather than being duplicated here.

## STOP boundaries and beta honesty

Stop advancement and escalate within the applicable authority when evidence
indicates any of the following:

- secret leakage or authorization violation;
- shared-state corruption or deck loss;
- unrecoverable desynchronization;
- inability to complete the core player journey.

Rare Comprehensive Rules edges with an honest guided or manual fallback, and
minor polish outside the active target, may remain beta limitations. They must
not be represented as completed automation or used to conceal failure of the
core journey.

## Compact reporting

Report the player-visible outcome and evidence first. Then report changed
files, targeted validation, independent-audit status, deferred limitations,
and any unresolved STOP condition. Keep supporting governance, audit,
telemetry, and substrate activity separately identified so it cannot be
mistaken for delivered player value.

## Boundary with product requirements

This policy defines how the authorized product outcome is pursued and judged.
The companion product requirements document alone defines why the product
exists, what player outcome and shared UI system it must provide, its Display
A/B roles, comparative quality target, accessibility, and non-goals.
